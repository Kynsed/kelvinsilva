What you get

A human second opinion on top of your platform's free scan. I log in as two of your users and try to read and change each other's data, and in the full audit I test your payments and webhooks.

You get a plain-English report ranked by severity, with the exact fix for each. Want them fixed too? I do that at a fixed price.
